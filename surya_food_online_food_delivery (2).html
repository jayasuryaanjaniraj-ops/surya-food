Who We Are

About SURYA FOOD

SURYA FOOD is an online food-delivery platform based in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh. Our goal is to make delicious food accessible to everyone by connecting customers with great restaurants and popular dishes. We focus on convenience, food variety, quality service and a smooth ordering experience.

Our Mission

To deliver fresh, hygienic, and flavorful meals from Vijayawada's finest kitchens straight to your doorstep in record time.

Our Vision

To become Andhra Pradesh's most trusted, customer-first food delivery brand empowering local restaurants and delighting families daily.
